## FAQ

**Can I study with CourseWeave or evaluate it locally at work?** Yes, subject to
the license. No registration or CourseWeave license fee is required.

**Can I build a product with it?** A noncompeting product may be permitted. A
competing hosted, white-label, or embedded platform is outside the standard
license, whether paid or free.

**Does this license apply to the course or my independent work?** The course has
its own licenses, and independently authored learner work remains the learner's.
Copies or modifications of CourseWeave or course components keep their applicable
license terms.

**Which text controls?** [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE). This FAQ only explains the
intended boundary in concise language.
